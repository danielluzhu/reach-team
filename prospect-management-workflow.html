Prospect Management Workflow

Home/Unit Information

Phase 1: Initial Outreach

  • Situation: A prospect inquires via a listing platform (e.g., Zillow, apartments.com, Furnished Finder, Facebook).
  • Goal: Verify core requirements and convert the inquiry into a scheduled tour.
  • Actionable Steps:
  • Confirm location, availability date, lease term, and rent alignment.
  • Provide a concise summary of the property features (e.g., private studio, mini-kitchen, laundry, bike storage).
  • Propose a specific tour time to minimize friction.
  • Communication Example:

"Hello! Thank you for your interest. These are new-build studios near UW and the light rail. Each includes a private bathroom and kitchenette. Units can come furnished for free for 9-12 month leases starting between now and September. Would you like to schedule a tour for Tuesday at 1:00 PM?"

Phase 2: Property Tour

  • Situation: The prospect arrives for a scheduled viewing.
  • Goal: Showcase the property effectively and secure a lease application.
  • Actionable Steps:
  • Tours should be scheduled within 1 day - ideally next hour - of initial message.
  • Schedule tours within 3 days of inquiry to maintain momentum.
  • Provide current tenants with at least 24 hours' notice for occupied units.
  • Highlight physical fit (layout, size, neighborhood) and operational details (maintenance, rent payment, pet policy).
  • Generally all questions fall under unit/building, lease details, and application
  • Answer all technical and lifestyle questions to facilitate an immediate application request.

Phase 3: Post-Tour Engagement

  • Situation: The tour has concluded, but the application has not yet been submitted.
  • 1: They liked tour, but have other places they want to see.
  • We are on their list but failed to compel them. We will need to stay on top of mind so they dont forget about us as a good option. We can keep them updated with the leasing situation (we have # units remaining)
  • 2: They did not like tour
  • We want to follow up and learn why, and see if theres something we missed.
  • This helps us not make the mistake in the future
  • Opportunity to divert them to similar listings that we have
  • Goal: Address remaining concerns and overcome objections to finalize the application.
  • Actionable Steps:
  • Identify core concerns (e.g., safety, location, or price) based on tour observations.
  • Emphasize features that directly address those concerns.
  • Mention flexibility on move-in incentives or rent proration if price is the primary barrier.
  • Notes:
  • We should expect tenants to be looking at multiple properties. It will help to proactively message them additional details we think they are missing.
  • We should also expect that if they are concerned about something, they may not say it unless we ask about it specifically. (e.g. if safety is a concern, “do you have any questions about the area, buildings security system, or otherwise?”)

Note: Once the application is submitted, Dan will manage the process through to lease signing.
